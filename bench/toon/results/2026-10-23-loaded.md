# 100 skinned foxes: pbr vs toon vs toon+ink (provisional)

`crates/bevy-ts-material/tests/toon_bench.rs`, release, Apple M5 Max, 1920x1080 offscreen,
600 frames after 300 of warm-up. Wall time per `app.update()`.

Not a measurement: the machine's load average was 34-45 throughout, so these numbers are noise.
Rerun on a quiet machine.

| arm | mean | p95 |
|---|---|---|
| pbr (StandardMaterial) | 13.83 ms | 41.89 ms |
| toon (shadows, no prepass) | 8.00 ms | 22.99 ms |
| toon + ink (depth and normal prepass) | 17.60 ms | 47.93 ms |
